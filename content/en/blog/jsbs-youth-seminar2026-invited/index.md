I will give an invited talk titled "Emotional Development and Parent–Child Interaction Based on Predictive Processing" at the Young Researchers Seminar hosted by the Youth Division of the Japan Society of Baby Science (JSBS), held on July 25–26, 2026, at the Toyonaka Campus of Osaka University.

The seminar aims to build a community where young researchers and practitioners interested in baby science can meet across disciplines, learn from one another, and grow their research together. It is jointly organized by the Society's Youth Division and Science & Engineering Division, and features invited talks, oral and poster presentations by young researchers, and participatory workshops.
