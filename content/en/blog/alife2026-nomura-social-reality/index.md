Kentaro Nomura of our lab will give the following presentation at the 2026 Conference on Artificial Life ([ALIFE 2026](https://2026.alife.org/), August 17–21, 2026, Waterloo, Canada), whose theme is "Living and Lifelike Complex Adaptive Systems."

<!--more-->

**[Social Reality Construction via Active Inference: Modeling the Dialectic of Conformity and Creativity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09026)**
*Kentaro Nomura (presenter), Takato Horii (Osaka Univ.)*
Wednesday, August 19, 2026, 11:00–11:20 (local time) — Room LH1011
Session: "Opinion, Society, Sociality" (Chairs: Steen Rasmussen, Rudolf M. Füchslin)

Using active inference as a unifying framework, this work models through multi-agent simulation how social reality is constituted from interactions among agents. Agents that form social priors over a structured network endogenously give rise to informationally cohesive groups through the dialectic of conformity and creativity, and a circular mutual constitution emerges between social representations and the observation distribution.

For details, see the [ALIFE 2026 website](https://2026.alife.org/) and [arXiv:2604.09026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09026).
