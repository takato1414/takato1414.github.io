*Affect Studies: Body and Affect* (in Japanese), edited by Mamoru Ito and Takeshi Arimoto, has been published by the University of Tokyo Press as Volume 2 of the series "Humanities of Materiality and Affect." Takato Horii co-authored Chapter 8, "**Emotion as a Semiotic Process Emerging from Interactions Inside and Outside the Body**," with Prof. Tadahiro Taniguchi (Kyoto University).

<!--more-->

The book explores affect as a "movement of life" that stirs beneath language and consciousness, bringing together perspectives from philosophy, anthropology, media studies, and early childhood education. It also includes a Japanese translation of Brian Massumi's landmark essay "The Autonomy of Affect."

Our chapter discusses emotion as a semiotic process that emerges from interactions inside and outside the body, offering a perspective from robotics and AI.
